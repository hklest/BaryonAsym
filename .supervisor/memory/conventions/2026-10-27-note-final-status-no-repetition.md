An analysis note is clean and compact and conveys only the final status. Each statement appears in one section; results sections do not re-explain methods or re-discuss earlier sections. No reference to superseded studies, earlier editions or retired approaches.
Why: the user found the same statements repeated across sections and wants no history in the note.
Rejected: cross-referencing or summarising earlier sections inside results; noting what an earlier study did.
