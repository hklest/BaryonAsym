### Method (acceptance-free; do not replace with a moment of the raw yield)

Per (charge, 4D bin) split tracks into 7 `phi_h` bins on `[-pi,pi]`; in each form the **helicity-difference ratio** `A_j = (N+_j - N-_j)/(N+_j + N-_j)`, in which helicity-independent acceptance cancels exactly; fit the 7 points with

    A_j = a0 + <|P|> * A_sin * sin(phi_j) + <|P|> * A_cos * cos(phi_j)

`A_sin` is the raw `A_LU^{sin phi_h}` (central), `A_cos` is the parity-forbidden null test, `a0` absorbs the helicity relative-luminosity imbalance. `phi_h` is Trento in the `gamma*+p` CM frame after the `W=q+P` boost. ### BSA traps — three real bugs found; do not reintroduce

1. **Never use a polarization-weighted moment of the raw yield** (`sum(P sin phi)/sum(P^2 sin^2 phi)`). It multiplies the *unpolarized* azimuthal acceptance by the mean polarization and produced a fake **9-sigma** `cos(phi)` signal. Use the phi-binned helicity difference.
2. **Regularize the binomial error.** Where one helicity state is empty in a phi bin the ratio saturates at `+-1`, its sigma collapses, and the bin gets infinite fit weight with `|a0| -> 1`. Fixed by Agresti-Coull plus `>=5` counts per helicity per phi bin and a condition-number cut.
