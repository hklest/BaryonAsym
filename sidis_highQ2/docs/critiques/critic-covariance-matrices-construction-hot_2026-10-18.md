## Fatal Issues

### F-1. D4 raw cross-block assembly is mixed-basis and non-PSD — confirmed UNSOUND; must not reach S9 (target 2b)

**Claim criticized:** the D4 default assembly grafts the S2 joint-toy cross-block covariance values onto the sidecar per-charge diagonal blocks.

**Evidence:** cold critic established `cross[0][0] = 3,422,449.87 counts^2` (18.9 sigma vs toy_se 181,254.50) seated next to sidecar marginals sqrt(1,837,453.82 x 1,712,776.56) = 1,774,225 -> rho_assembled = 1.929, 2x2 det -8.57e12, asymmetry variance -3.29e6 counts^2. This session verified the mechanism from construction records: `joint_toy_record.json` line 4 (lineage: joint toys "SUPERSEDE the SA-3 sidecar ... for the h+/h- CROSS-BLOCK ONLY; the per-charge sidecar blocks remain the TIER_A C_stat term") and the agreement headline: joint diag exceeds sidecar by **+5.06%/+4.89% median, +86.2%/+84.2% max on sigma scale** (`diag_rel_diff_median` 0.0506/0.0489; `diag_rel_excess_over_poisson_max` 1.146), note: "differences quantify the event-clustering excess over pure Poisson ...; publish-not-tune".

**Physical mechanism (confirmed):** the joint cross entries scale with the *joint* (event-cluster) marginals; the diagonal blocks are *sidecar* (Poisson) marginals. Where the joint/sidecar variance ratio is large, the grafted cross entry violates Cauchy-Schwarz. Decomposing rho_assembled = rho_joint x (sigma_joint/sigma_sidecar)^2: at the bin-0 pair, 1.929 / 0.745 (joint-basis rho, cold critic) => variance ratio ~ 2.59, i.e. +61% sigma — exactly the cold critic's bin-0 figure. So the non-PSD is *entirely* a marginal-scale inconsistency, not an unphysical joint correlation; the joint machinery itself is internally consistent.

**Adjudication of the two fixes (question 2):**
- **Fix (a) — re-base the cross-block via the joint correlation onto sidecar marginals, `cross' = rho_joint,cross o sigma_sc,pos sigma_sc,neg^T`: RECOMMENDED.** It restores entrywise Cauchy-Schwarz (|rho| <= 1 by construction), preserves the published sidecar diagonal and the D4 lineage mandate ("cross-block ONLY"), and makes the failing 2x2 minors valid (0.745 < 1; bin-0 asymmetry variance becomes positive). **However, full 490x490 PSD is NOT mathematically guaranteed for a block graft** — entrywise bounds do not imply the block-operator condition ||A^(-1/2) X B^(-1/2)|| <= 1 — so the S9 pre-assembly PSD gate is **mandatory**, with a **pre-registered fallback**: (i) scalar shrinkage of the cross-block toward zero until PSD (record the factor), or (ii) the full-matrix Schur re-base `rho_joint,full o sigma_sc sigma_sc^T`, which is PSD-guaranteed (Schur product theorem: sample correlation from 1000 toys is PSD; rank-1 sigma sigma^T is PSD) but *replaces the sidecar within-charge correlation structure with the joint one* — a deviation from the D4 lineage text requiring explicit supervisor sign-off.
- **Fix (b) — supersede D4 with the joint per-charge blocks: NOT recommended.** It moves the published C_stat diagonal (+5.06%/+4.89% sigma median, up to +86% max; ~+10% variance median), breaking consistency with the release CSV `toy_stat_unc`, the S1 `mag_ibu_stat_rel` custody column, and S8's budget reconciliation (an ibu_stat deviation with no named convention cause = S8 FAIL by its own rule). Legitimate only as a release-level re-publication decision, outside this package.
- **Additional guard:** the re-based cross-block inherits correlations involving the 45 ever-clipped fine bins (clip report: 2,890 instances, 967/1000 toys, top bin clipped in 379/1000 toys, 14 guarded bins) — one-sided clipping distorts those correlations; flag or check them at the PSD gate.

**Impact:** analysis-killing if assembled raw (negative eigenvalues, negative variances, chi2/inversion failure); fully fixable via (a) + gate.

## Major Concerns

### M-1. C_stat released diagonal is a measured *lower bound* in cluster-heavy bins — publish-not-tune must become a prominent S9 register entry (target 1b, question 1)

The variance *split* is sound and disjoint — spec lines 390/741-746: "toy/released statistical input is Poisson-only (w2 = data_sub); template variance carried by bgsub_stat_rel (no double-count; retired rev-2 defect = Poisson added twice, data variance x2.00022)", with exactly-once E1b law, STOP on double-Poisson, asserts `w2` bitwise == data_sub' and `negative_bins == 0`, and the distinctness statement (spec line 1107). No double-count, no omission. **But** the same S2 study that the package commissioned measured the model deficit of that convention: event-clustered data over-disperses bin counts vs independent Poisson (multi-track events), so the sidecar diagonal understates the stat uncertainty by +5.06%/+4.89% median and up to +86%/+84% sigma in cluster-heavy bins; 4.01%/4.00% of sidecar covariance entries exceed the 3-sigma agreement tolerance (`sidecar_agreement.json`, "publish-not-tune"). A bin-level Poisson toy also *cannot* generate a pos/neg cross-block (zero by construction) — an assumption S2 has now falsified (18.9-sigma cross entries).

**Why it matters:** downstream consumers reading C_stat as "the statistical uncertainty" get a known lower bound in the tails. The convention is pre-registered and legitimate (changing it is a release-level decision), but only if the S9 package carries the caveat *at the C_stat term*, with the S2 numbers — otherwise the convention silently degrades into an unqualified claim. **Must-fix (documentation register, not a number change).**
