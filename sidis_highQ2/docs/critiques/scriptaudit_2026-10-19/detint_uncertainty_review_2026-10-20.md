## 2. Model-internal vs model-validity determination

**(a) The detint legs are MODEL-INTERNAL variations. DECISIVE.** Both RAPGAP31 and DJANGOH14 responses are produced through the **same H1 detector simulation**; the "model" leg varies only the generator feeding that sim, and the "stat" leg is MC-statistics of the parentage fraction. The G1-G13 gate suite (PASS 13/13) validating the S2' variant is **entirely MC-internal** (identities, class partitions, IBU closure, 1000-toy Poisson closure, per-bin removable-fraction consistency vs the parentage map) — no gate compares to data, and the spec states data-side per-track separation is impossible (flags MC-only; the only data-side discriminator is |DcaPrime| survival: det.int. 90.2% vs signal 99.5%). Therefore: **varying knobs inside the same detector simulation cannot cover detector-model validity, and no budget term is a detector-model-validity term.** The frozen v6sys source menu (v6sys_h1_precedent_systematics_manifest_2026-06-03.md) contains no data-MC scale-factor, material-budget, or dE/dx source. The nearest partial, indirect coverage: tracking_eff_m0p5 (0.51%/0.52%, -0.5% random data-side track drop, 16 seeds — with OPEN caveats: no full-chain tracking variant, charge-dependent-tracking convention open), the five calibration-shift legs (sensitivity within the MC model), ibu_model (generator spread, B1-floored), phi_h_gauss (acceptance azimuthal shape), and — **in the covariance package only, NOT in the budget CSV** — the response body/miss MC-stat term C_MCstat (measured medians 0.853%/0.920%, K=200 replicas).

**(b) Size evidence for detector-model error.** The dedicated data-MC artifact is docs/qa/DATA_MC_RECO_YIELD_DISCREPANCY_QA_2026-10-09.md (h1-qa-specialist, verdict PASS on its audit question): a real ~6% event-level / **12-13% track-level MC deficit** vs data, adjudicated **generator-physics (model-level), not a selection/weighting/scaling bug** (byte-identical code path; no single scale factor fits events and tracks — 8.8%/6.4% mutually inconsistent; strongly shape-dependent 0.23->1.02 and generator-specific; reproduced through four independent paths). Two data-side facts bound the detector-sim component: unweighted MC track multiplicities **bracket** data (dj 0.997x, rg 1.030x) — excluding a gross MC tracking-efficiency error >~3% — and track-quality pass fractions agree to **<=1.6 pp** (dj -0.14 pp, rg +0.80 pp). However, the QA does **not** quantify a detector-simulation residual: a common-mode detector-sim component shared by both generators is not excluded by construction, and the 12-13% common deficit is far above ibu_model (~1.4% covers only the RG-DJ spread). Chain QA gates (2026-10-07 PASS-WITH-CONDITIONS; G1-G13; cone 18/18) are internal-validity gates, not data-MC detector validation. Register entries that explicitly carry the uncovered residue: **trigger = UNEVALUATED_PLACEHOLDER ("no data/MC comparison product exists; not in the budget; never assumed zero")**; **S7 prior-reweight = REPORT_ONLY_UNPROPAGATED (medians 8.82%/9.54% exceeding the budget totals; eps/p_eff attribution PLAUSIBLE-NOT-PROVEN)**; charge-dependent tracking OPEN_CAVEAT; NucliaWeight r_min proxy (0.98167) bounded caveat — NucliaWeight being precisely the material-interaction correction (mean 0.9895/0.9897).

**(c) RG31-vs-DJ14 in the budget.** The generator crosscheck enters three ways: ibu_model_rel (medians 1.40%/1.43%, max 17.7%/18.2% — ~48x the detint legs; B1 replica floor binds 152/167 bins); detint_removal_model_rel (0.03%); and the **new** straddle_residual_rel (full RG/DJ envelope of the per-cell net straddle rate — added in the OPT-A driver, size TBD). The DJ detector-shift legs exist as **diagnostics only**. So a generator-independence check on the response exists and dominates the detint legs — but it is a *generator* check, not a *detector-model* check (same detector sim in both legs). On the "substandard RAPGAP31" premise: the artifacts document RG31 **provenance/exposure/binning** defects (NONRAD e- exposure repaired, emfix +53,773.7 nb^-1; xFcut manifest one-line stub, non-remediable; the N1 gen-Q2 straddle 1.94%/up to 8.74% per cell, cured by OPT-A; the resolved plain-prior mismatch) — **no artifact demonstrates an RG31-specific detector-physics deficiency**, and the detector sim is common to both generators. What *is* established is the user's deeper point: no data-MC detector-validity term exists anywhere in the budget.

## 3. VERDICT: **ADEQUATE-WITH-GAPS**

- **Detint-specific handling: ADEQUATE.** The correction is small (integrated ~0.11%; per-bin increment ~0.17% median, worst bins O(0.6-1%)); the assigned legs (removal generator spread 0.030% (+) fake-column MC-stat 0.34%) are derived, gated (G1-G13, g9 identities dev ~1e-16), and of the same order as any plausible detector-model-induced error on the removal — even a 100% error in the GEANT material-interaction description of the removable class would contribute ~0.11% integrated / ~0.2% per-bin median, below the family's current max and ~2% of total budget variance at median. The detint legs are 0.15% of the per-bin budget variance (median); they cannot dominate any quoting decision.
- **General detector-model validity: a real, disclosed GAP.** No term covers it; the 12-13% common data-MC deficit is attributed (high confidence, not proof) to generator physics and covered by nothing if that attribution is incomplete; trigger is unevaluated; the response body/miss MC-stat (0.85%/0.92%) is measured but missing from the budget CSV; S7 (~9%) is unpropagated with unproven attribution. The register's never-assumed-zero discipline makes this an *honest* gap, not a silent one — defensible for internal quoting once C1 lands, but it must be disclosed in any publication and is best narrowed in the regeneration window.

## 5. Why bgsub gets a flat 100% while detint gets derived legs — justified, with one caveat

The e/V0 terms are **subtractive contaminants** applied to the data vector pre-unfold, with magnitudes from data-side templates whose error has no derivable model — the honest envelope for an unmeasured subtraction magnitude is +/-100% of the subtracted content, fully correlated, and it automatically covers any detector-model error in the template magnitude/shape. The detint removal is a **response-internal (multiplicative-structure) correction** anchored in MC truth parentage, with a *measurable* generator spread (RG and DJ agree on the increment to 0.03% per bin; DJ/RG f_i-growth ratio 1.099/1.097) and quantifiable MC-stat — so a small derived uncertainty is the honest choice; the asymmetry correctly places the crude 100% conservatism where the magnitude is largest and least known (env100 median 3.16%/3.56%, dominating the budget) and derived legs where the model spread is measured (0.15% of budget variance). The caveat: the derived detint legs validate the removal **only against the other generator**, never against data (impossible per-track); the 100% bgsub envelope incidentally covers detector-model error while the detint legs do not — recommendation 2 closes exactly that residue.

4. S7 attribution unproven; RNG seed unrecorded; ~9% medians ride as report-only.

6. straddle_residual_rel size unknown until the OPT-A driver completes; its documented common-mode QED blindness and partial overlap with ibu_model remain limitations.
7. The env100 builder (M1) remains unlocated; the OPT-A C1-fix driver is the reconstruction path but was still mid-completion (STEP 18+ output section) at review time.

**Bottom line:** the detint uncertainty is a model-internal, generator-plus-MC-stat derivation — defensible in size for the small (~0.11% integrated) correction it governs, and it is the *smallest* family in the budget (0.15% of per-bin variance at median). The defensible-but-incomplete part is that detector-model *validity* is nowhere carried as a systematic: it is disclosed through the register (trigger, S7, tracking caveats, C_MCstat living outside the budget) and partially absorbed by calibration legs, generator crosschecks, and the 0.5% tracking term. The OPT-A regeneration window already fixes C1 and adds the straddle-residual home; the four cheap additions above (C_MCstat into the budget, explicit detint material-validity bound, DJ detector-leg envelope, disclosure discipline) would convert the remaining implicit assumptions into explicit, bounded terms without new production.
