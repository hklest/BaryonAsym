### MINOR-2 (B3, covlegs stage-3) — PLUS/MINUS leg grouping is custodial, not physical direction; JES sign would invert a naive signed delta
**Claim criticized:** the covlegs driver groups PLUS_LEGS = {hfs_jes_down, hfs_rces_up, hfs_theta_up}, MINUS_LEGS = {hfs_jes_up, hfs_rces_down, hfs_theta_down} (L106-108), and the docstring frames S5f signed deltas as `delta_k = (U_plus − U_minus)/2`. **Evidence:** the campaign's own validator carries explicit direction tags — `validate_smoke_covlegs.py` L84-88: `hfs_jes_up: +1, hfs_rces_down: −1, hfs_theta_down: −1`, with the comment "mirrors the plus-leg precedent directions jes_down/−1, rces_up/+1, theta_up/+1". So plus/minus = banked-vs-new campaign custody, not lever direction; for JES the grouping is direction-inverted relative to rces/theta. **Why it matters physically:** a downstream consumer interpreting `(U_plus − U_minus)/2` as the physical +1σ JES shift gets the JES sign flipped; sign errors in signed systematics propagate silently. **Mitigation (why MINOR, not MAJOR):** leg names are self-describing and recorded in every manifest; direction tags exist in the validators; all stage-3 outputs audited here are unsigned comps (`comp = |leg − nom|/nom`); the stated S5f purpose (sign-symmetry record) is insensitive to the odd-part sign. **Impact:** follow-up only for the audited drivers; decisive for the unaudited S5f consumer.
