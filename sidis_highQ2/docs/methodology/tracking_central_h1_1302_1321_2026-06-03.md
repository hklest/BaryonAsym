This source applies the *exact previous H1 published tracking practice* to
the high-Q² inclusive charged-hadron SIDIS analysis (no PID).

Reference: H1 Collaboration, "Measurement of charged particle spectra in
deep inelastic ep scattering at HERA", arXiv:1302.1321 / Eur. Phys. J. C

## Fiducial rationale

The high-Q² SIDIS reconstruction selection uses hadron tracks with
`|η_h| < 1.3`, strictly inside the central jet chamber (CTD)
acceptance `|η| < 1.5` used in arXiv:1302.1321 §5. Therefore only the
central-tracks row applies. The 10% combined / forward-track
uncertainty from that section is **excluded by construction** because
no forward tracks enter the high-Q² SIDIS sample.

## Explicit exclusions

The following are *not* included in this source and are either covered
by separate dedicated sources or are orthogonal mechanisms:

- 10% combined / forward-track term (forward tracks not used).
- `eElecTr*` scattered-electron tracking variations (electron-side
  source).
- `H1CalcTrackDowngrade` shifts (orthogonal calculator-level
  mechanism).
- Track momentum scale (separate source).
- Track momentum resolution (separate source).
- `H1CalcSystematic` shifts in general (this source is *not* an
  `H1Calc` variation).
- MC shifted-response sources (no re-unfolding performed).
- v6sys treebuilder variations (no rebuild performed).

## Mathematical form

- Stage: **post-unfolding**, applied to per-bin central unfolded values.
- For each charge `c ∈ {h+, h-}` and bin `i`:
  ```
  sigma_i^{(c)} = sigma_rel * | x_i^{(c)} |
  Cov_ii^{(c)}  = (sigma_i^{(c)})^2
  Cov_ij^{(c)}  = 0   for i != j
  ```
- The h+ and h- blocks are **independent**. The cross-charge covariance
  is **zero** by construction (the same per-track scalar is treated as
  independently applied to h+ and h- and the cross-block is reported as
  0.0; no cancellation in the charge asymmetry `A_h` is assumed).
- Bin-to-bin covariance is **diagonal only**.

  satisfied: 157 finalbin, 210 raw bins).
- `README.md`: human-readable summary next to the artifact.

## Validation

The emitter runs the following gates and refuses to write if any fail:

1. Per-bin relative sigma equals the canonical `sigma_rel` exactly
   wherever the central value is nonzero (atol `1e-15`).
2. `cov_diag[i] == sigma[i]**2` element-wise.
3. `sigma[i] >= 0` everywhere.
4. Zero sigma iff zero central (no spurious sigma at empty bins).
5. When the full matrix is stored, all off-diagonal entries are exactly
   `0.0` (asserted).

An independent post-emit verification re-derived sigma from the stored
central values, checked covariance diagonality, and confirmed the
canonical constant matched byte-for-byte against
`0.01019803902718557`. Result: all gates pass on both candidate
releases. Max numerical residual: `1.73e-18` (floating-point noise
only).

## How combiners must consume this source

- Treat as `UNCORRELATED-ACROSS-BINS, FULLY-CORRELATED-WITHIN-A-BIN`.
- Contributes only to the diagonal of each per-charge block of the
  total post-unfolding covariance.
- The cross-charge block is zero; **do not** assume cancellation in
  `A_h`. The asymmetry-Jacobian propagation of the h+ and h- diagonals
  yields a strictly positive contribution to `Cov(A_h)`.
