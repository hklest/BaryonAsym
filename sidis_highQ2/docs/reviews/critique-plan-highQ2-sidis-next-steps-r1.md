# Critique: plan-highQ2-sidis-next-steps r1 (physics-correctness review)

**Verdict:** `CONCERN`

## Material reviewed

- Observable class: charge-separated SIDIS multiplicities `M^{h+/-}(Q2, x, z, pT_h)`, hadron charge asymmetry `A_h`, and optional azimuthal moments `A1 cos(phi_h)`, `A2 cos(2 phi_h)` in the high-Q2 regime (`Q2 > 150 GeV2`).
- Channel: H1 inclusive charged-hadron SIDIS, high-Q2, ep0607 currently in hand; e- periods (`em0405`, `em06`) and `ep0304` documented as missing.
- Analysis family: SIDIS (charged-hadron).
- Evidence type: a planning artifact (gating tree G1-G17 with tasks and risk register), the supporting research snapshot (2026-05-19), the shared SIDIS handoff (acting as embedded supervisor anchors), the high-Q2 SIDIS extension research, and the SIDIS systematics reference playbook. No bin-resolved numbers, code snippets, or per-source per-bin tables were supplied; this is a methodology-and-procedure critique of the plan only.

Anchors used as priors: data luminosity the superseded pre-rescale luminosity nb^-1 (low-Q2; the high-Q2 recoverable S67 total is 356,613 nb^-1 per the snapshot), `weight1 * weight2`, MC luminosity cancels in acceptance, no BR correction when the gen denominator covers all decay channels, acceptance in (0,1], asymmetries in [-1,1], standard `C_rad` ~ 0.7-0.8, K0s asymmetry ~ 0.

## Fatal Issues

### F1. Multiplicity formula is never stated; bin-width division is never required at the gate level

- **Claim being criticized.** G9 ("Multiplicity extraction") and the systematics gate G11 are built on top of a multiplicity definition that is nowhere in the plan. The gate text says only "extract preliminary high-Q2 `M^{h+}` and `M^{h-}` in approved bins, using the frozen high-Q2 nominal chain". The Tasks section repeats this. There is no statement that the implementation must realize `M^{h+/-}(Q2,x,z,pT_h) = N_h_corr / (N_DIS_corr * Dz * DpT_h)` and there is no requirement that the bin widths `Dz` and `DpT_h` (or `Dx` if integrated differently) be divided out.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 156-162 (G9 exit criterion), lines 430-445 (G9 tasks), lines 593-598 (open questions on dimensionality). Risk register lines 575-588 has no entry for "multiplicity normalized as a count rather than a density."
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Without enforced bin-width division, the "multiplicity" produced is `N_h/N_DIS` per bin, not `dN_h/(N_DIS dz dpT_h)`. Across non-uniform bin widths (the plan explicitly contemplates rebinning at G5 and a 3D fallback at the user-decision stage in question 5) this gives a result that does NOT integrate back to total multiplicity, cannot be compared to HERMES/COMPASS/H1-DESY-20-176 published shapes, and will mislead any TMD fit consumer. Also, model-swap and cut-scan deltas computed on the un-normalized object versus the density-normalized object differ by the bin-width Jacobian per source, silently changing systematic ratios.
- **Severity.** Analysis-killing for any publication-grade multiplicity table. The plan must require the formula explicitly at G9 and the bin-width divisor as part of the G9 exit criterion, not as something assumed inherited from low-Q2 prose. Note that the low-Q2 handoff itself does not write the formula either (the closest reference is the systematics reference cookbook).

### F2. Zero-hadron DIS denominator is treated as a prose precaution, not as a numerical gate

- **Claim being criticized.** G4 says the `N_DIS` denominator "explicitly includes zero-hadron events" and G7 says the DIS-event acceptance denominator "includes zero-hadron DIS events". The Tasks for G4.2 and G7.2 produce a "zero-hadron DIS check" artifact (`highQ2_zero_hadron_dis_check.json`). However, the exit criterion of neither gate states what the artifact must contain numerically. There is no required equality check such as `N(DisTot entries) = N(events with charged track) + N(events with zero charged track in fiducial)`, nor a threshold on the zero-hadron fraction, nor a requirement to compare event counts taken from `DisTot` directly to event counts inferred by collapsing `ChargedTot` (which is the canonical trap when developers accidentally derive `N_DIS` from the track tree).
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 116-122 (G4 exit criterion), lines 140-146 (G7 exit criterion), lines 355-370 (G4 tasks), lines 406-416 (G7 tasks), lines 660-661 (Todos for G7), and risk register row "`eps_DIS` denominator missing zero-hadron events" at line 580 -- which is correctly named as `high` severity but whose mitigation again only says "require explicit zero-hadron validation artifact" with no numerical pass condition.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** If even a few percent of DIS events with zero charged hadrons are dropped from `N_DIS`, the multiplicity is biased high by exactly that fraction, uniformly across all (z, pT_h) bins -- an irreducible normalization-shape distortion that masquerades as overall yield agreement at moderate z and amplifies at high z where multiplicity is naturally lower. This is the single most common analysis-killing mistake in inclusive SIDIS multiplicity work and is explicitly named in this critic's system prompt as a known trap. The plan recognizes the trap but does not arm the gate with a numerical exit criterion that would catch it.
- **Severity.** Analysis-killing if missed; demoted to FATAL because the gate as written would pass on a check that merely "exists" rather than one that demonstrates inclusion.

### F3. Charge-symmetric acceptance is not forbidden in the gate; A_h cancellation logic for non-charge-cancelling sources is unspecified

- **Claim being criticized.** G6 ("Hadron acceptance") only requires `0 < eps_h <= 1` per retained bin. It does not require that `eps_{h+}(bin) != eps_{h-}(bin)` is checked, nor does it require that the asymmetry formula must always be built from per-charge corrected yields (`A_h = (N+_corr - N-_corr)/(N+_corr + N-_corr)`) and never from a single common acceptance applied to (N+ - N-) and (N+ + N-) separately. The Tasks for G6.3 ("plot charge-separated acceptance maps and acceptance ratios to expose charge-dependent detector effects early") is the only soft hint; nothing in G6, G9, or G11 enforces the per-charge-first-then-asymmetry rule.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 132-138 (G6 exit criterion), lines 389-404 (G6 tasks). For A_h cancellation: G11 exit criterion at lines 172-179 names "cancellation classes for `A_h` are recorded separately" but does not require per-charge propagation. Task G11.3 (line 470) says "Record which sources cancel" but does not require that for any non-cancelling source the variation must be computed h+ and h- separately and the asymmetry rebuilt -- which is exactly the operational rule the systematics reference (lines 355-358 of `reference-sidis-systematics.md`) calls out: "do not subtract h+ and h- systematic bands in quadrature assuming full cancellation".
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Charged hadrons interact differently with detector material (the K- absorption channel and antiproton annihilation channel are much larger than their charge conjugates), and tracking efficiency has a charge-asymmetric component. If acceptance is silently mirrored or if A_h systematics are propagated by quadrature-subtracting two charge bands rather than recomputed per shifted-charge sample, the resulting A_h is biased by a charge-asymmetric detector residual whose sign is not even guaranteed. Lambda-anchor expectation `A ~ +0.054` and proton-anchor expectations cannot be used here, but DESY 20-176 + ZEUS high-Q2 prior expect A_h at the percent level; a charge-asymmetric tracking residual at the 0.2% level wrong-signs the leading bin trivially. The plan's silence on the operational rule lets the analysis-killer through.
- **Severity.** Analysis-killing for A_h. Even given the explicit publication block on A_h (G15), the plan still authorizes internal A_h diagnostics in G11.3 and again in G15.3 ("Confirm that the recovered coverage is sufficient to enable beam-charge / period-split systematics for A_h"), so the per-charge-first rule must be a gate, not an aspiration.

## Major Concerns

### M1. Purity >= 0.5 cutoff is stated as a global rule with no per-bin enforcement, no closure exit criterion, and no migration-regime test

- **Claim being criticized.** G8 says "bin-by-bin is allowed only where purity >= 0.5, otherwise unfolding is required." G12 says reuse `tmd_unfold.py` and "compare bin-by-bin and unfolded results in the bins flagged for unfolding". Nowhere is there an exit gate that the unfolding has been refolded and matches the input pseudodata to a stated tolerance, nor that a closure pull distribution is required, nor that regularization strength has been scanned (the systematics reference cookbook names `unfolding_reg_scan` explicitly at line 326 but the plan does not include this scan in G12 or G11).
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 148-154 (G8 exit), lines 180-186 (G12 exit), lines 418-428 (G8 tasks), lines 481-491 (G12 tasks). Compared to the SIDIS handoff which records the low-Q2 result that "Scheme A median 4D purity 0.52" and that "z is the binding dimension" and that "A_h needs coarser binning (192 bins) than multiplicities" (handoff lines 1031-1041): high-Q2 will have lower per-bin statistics by a factor of 3-5 (per `research-highQ2-sidis-extension.md` Section 6.5), so purity is likely to be in the 0.3-0.6 range across many bins, making the unfolding decision the dominant correction-method choice.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** A bin-by-bin correction applied where migration is strong induces shape distortion that exactly looks like a TMD pT broadening signal at high pT_h and a fragmentation peak shift at moderate z -- precisely the observables the analysis is selling. A purity >= 0.5 threshold without a closure test does not guarantee the bin-by-bin correction is unbiased; it only guarantees it is "not the worst case." Without unfolded closure / refolding as a hard exit gate, the multiplicity result inherits the migration regime as a hidden systematic.
- **Severity.** Major concern, not fatal: the plan does require unfolding for bins below the threshold, but the threshold is too weak and the closure exit is missing.

### M2. C_rad placeholder is permitted to ship in the internal package without a stated upper bound on the placeholder bias

- **Claim being criticized.** G10 exit criterion says "either a real high-Q2 `C_rad` product exists or an explicit blocked placeholder status is recorded." G16 ("First internal high-Q2 convergence package") only requires "G9 complete; G11 complete; G13 complete if azimuthal moments are included." G10 is not in the G16 dependency list. The risk register has no row for "internal package shipped with C_rad = 1 placeholder."
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 164-170 (G10), lines 221-227 (G16), lines 244-263 (per-gate dependency graph), risk register lines 575-588. The supporting research (snapshot Section 7 item 7 and extension Section 2.6) says `C_rad = 1` is the current placeholder for both regimes. Anchor prior: typical SIDIS `C_rad ~ 0.7-0.8`.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** A placeholder `C_rad = 1` mis-states multiplicities by 20-30% at low Q2 and roughly 5-15% at the high-Q2 boundary (Q2 = 150-300 GeV2 region where radiative effects on the electron leg remain non-trivial), with a strong (Q2, y) dependence. The plan documents this as a status but does not require the internal package to either (a) cite a Q2- and y-dependent placeholder uncertainty band, or (b) restrict the internal package's claim space to ratios where C_rad cancels, or (c) propagate a `crad_model` placeholder systematic with a justified upper bound. The result is an internal package that, even within its blocked-from-publication scope, will be used as a reference number by downstream consumers (other H1 analyses, TMD-fit liaisons) and biased by a single-source effect that is by construction not in the error budget.
- **Severity.** Major. Demotes G10 from "may proceed in parallel" status to "must precede the G16 internal package," and adds an explicit C_rad-blocked-systematic placeholder requirement to G11.

### M3. LAr electron-scale procedure is named but not concretely specified; the SpaCal-import trap is named but not gated

- **Claim being criticized.** G11 lists "LAr-specific electron-scale" as a required source. Task G11.2 says "Define and execute the high-Q2 electron-reconstruction source using an LAr-specific procedure rather than the low-Q2 SpaCal post-hoc recipe", with agent listed as `supervisor decision required`. The risk register row "Mistaken reuse of low-Q2 SpaCal electron-scale procedure" (line 585) is `high` severity; its mitigation is "Treat LAr electron source as a dedicated high-Q2 adaptation item; require separate procedure in G11." But the plan never specifies the procedure (z-impact-region binning per the systematics reference and the extension research at Section 4.2), never specifies whether an evls-stage re-run is required versus a post-hoc proxy, and never specifies a numerical pass criterion for "LAr-specific" -- so the gate could be satisfied by a single +-1% shift labeled "LAr" and would still let the analysis-killing import slip through.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 172-179 (G11 exit), lines 459-479 (G11 tasks). Compare with `reference-sidis-systematics.md` lines 150, 183, 220-227, 229 (explicit prohibition on numerical transfer; 1-5% position-dependent shift across 5 z-impact regions), and `research-highQ2-sidis-extension.md` Section 4.2 and Section 6.6 (evls re-run requirement; 1.4k jobs scaled). Open question `OQ3 / HB7` flagged in snapshot Section 7 item 6 as "unresolved" -- the plan does not name OQ3 or HB7 anywhere.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** A 1% scalar electron-energy shift propagated post-hoc to LAr (the SpaCal recipe) under-counts the high-z-impact-region scale uncertainty by a factor of ~5 (since the LAr scale uncertainty reaches 5% at the extreme z bin per the LAr position-dependent calibration) and over-counts the central-impact region. The error then mis-states the dominant detector systematic on multiplicities by a factor of 2-5, asymmetrically across the (Q2, x) grid because the z-impact of the electron correlates with event Q2. The plan recognizes the trap by name but does not arm the gate to catch it; "supervisor decision required" is not a gate.
- **Severity.** Major. The plan needs an explicit OQ3 closure as a hard gate G11 precondition (or a stated alternate plan, e.g., bound-it-from-above with a +-5% scalar shift and propagate as a placeholder).

### M4. Trigger systematic and TrigWeightAC propagation are not gated for verification

- **Claim being criticized.** G4 freezes "the high-Q2 trigger semantics around S67 data-only selection and `TrigWeightAC` weighting." G11 lists "trigger verification" as a tracked-blocked source if "not yet closed." But the plan never requires a closure test that verifies the trigger weight propagates correctly into the multiplicity numerator (data) without entering the MC acceptance denominator. The systematics reference (lines 156, 328) names `trigger_verification` and `S67` explicitly; the plan does not give a numerical sanity check (e.g., monitor-trigger cross-check, or a comparison of multiplicity in two disjoint trigger-prescale buckets).
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 116-122 (G4 exit), lines 355-370 (G4 tasks), lines 459-479 (G11 tasks).
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Trigger-weight mis-propagation would shift data yields uniformly by a few percent across (Q2, x) (which appears as an overall normalization that partially absorbs into the MC-luminosity-vs-data-luminosity comparison at G14 -- making G14 falsely PASS); within-bin trigger-prescale variations would mimic a Q2-dependent multiplicity slope; TrigWeightAC accidentally applied to MC would silently re-scale acceptance.
- **Severity.** Major. The plan should add a "trigger weight applied only to data; not applied to MC; demonstrated by a parallel-no-weight reference" exit criterion to G4.

### M5. The plan never separates the case "G14 + G15 both fail" from "G14 passes, G15 fails" in the A_h systematic logic

- **Claim being criticized.** G15 exit criterion item 5 says "the period-split and beam-charge checks required for A_h are executable." G11 task 4 includes "period split before G15" as a tracked blocked source. But the plan never says that A_h evaluated on ep0607-only is **biased**, not merely **imprecise**: with zero e- exposure, the measured "A_h" entangles hadron-charge asymmetry with the lepton-beam-charge asymmetry of the underlying photoproduction kinematics. The plan's framing throughout (e.g., G15 risk register entry "A_h released from ep0607 only" with mitigation "Keep A_h blocked for publication until G15") implies the issue is publication policy. It is also a physics-correctness issue.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 208-219 (G15), lines 459-479 (G11), risk register line 578 (ep0607 only). Compare snapshot Section 4.2: `A_h on ep0607-only is technically computable but is not a meaningful physical asymmetry result since it would fold beam-charge asymmetry into hadron-charge asymmetry`. The plan re-uses this prose context but does not propagate the implication into the systematics block.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** With only e+p in the sample, the partonic process is dominated by `e+ q -> e+ q` with valence-u-quark dominance and a different antiquark contribution than the e-p case. This produces a non-trivial physical h+/h- asymmetry that **is** charge-asymmetric, but is not the same observable as the symmetric-beam A_h. Reporting the ep0607-only number as "A_h" mis-labels the observable; even quoting it as "preliminary A_h with caveat" suggests to a reader that the only fix is statistics, not lepton-beam coverage.
- **Severity.** Major. The plan should rename the ep0607-only quantity (e.g., `A_h^{e+p}`) wherever it appears as an internal diagnostic, and require the bias-not-just-precision statement in the G15 status file.

### M6. Correlated-uncertainty treatment between shared MC samples is not required

- **Claim being criticized.** G11 produces per-source deltas. There is no statement that the MC sample used for the nominal acceptance and the MC sample used for each cut-scan, model-swap, and shifted-MC systematic are statistically correlated (because they are largely the same events) and that the per-source deltas must be combined with explicit covariance, not in quadrature as independent sources. Likewise, multi-method unfolding regularization variations applied to the same response object are correlated and must not be quadratured.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 172-179 (G11), lines 480-491 (G12 tasks). Compare `reference-sidis-systematics.md` lines 347-349 (quadrature rule) and lines 355-358 (A_h per-charge propagation rule) -- the reference itself recommends quadrature, but for sources that share an MC sample this is incorrect for the **statistical** component of the per-source delta, and can also be wrong for the systematic component (e.g., model-swap and acceptance variation on the same RAPGAP base).
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Quadrature-sum of correlated deltas overestimates the total systematic when sources are positively correlated and underestimates it when negatively correlated; in either case, the reported error band is wrong by an O(1) factor for the dominant sources (model swap, cut scans, electron scale all touch the same RAPGAP/DJANGOH events), and the reported significance of any deviation from a TMD baseline is therefore biased.
- **Severity.** Major. The plan should require G11 to deliver a covariance matrix or, at minimum, a "shared-sample correlation flag" per source pair, and require G12 to deliver an unfolding covariance, not only diagonal errors.

### M7. G14 ("normalization validation") exit criteria are procedural, not numerical

- **Claim being criticized.** G14 lists five passing requirements: frozen weight expression, effective MC luminosity calculation, closure between expected and observed on a control quantity, written confirmation of consistency, and a PASS verdict. None of these specifies the numerical pass criterion -- what tolerance on the closure between expected and observed counts is acceptable (1%? 5%?), what the "validated control quantity" is (inclusive DIS cross-section vs published H1? DIS-event yield in (Q2, y) cells vs RAPGAP prediction scaled to data luminosity?), what value of effective MC luminosity is expected, what to do if the closure passes at one Q2 but fails at another. The gate as written can be claimed PASS by hand-waving on either side.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 196-206 (G14), lines 510-520 (G14 tasks), open question 3 (lines 594-596) implicitly acknowledges the ambiguity by asking the user whether G14 failure should trigger more normalization research.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** A loose G14 lets an incorrect H1CalcWeight integration through (e.g., wrong beam-energy normalization, double-counting of TrigWeightAC, miscount of effective MC luminosity over per-run files) and the resulting absolute multiplicity tables would be off by an unspecified normalization that downstream consumers cannot disentangle from physics. The known anchor "MC luminosity cancels in acceptance" only protects the ratio piece; the absolute scale comes from `N_DIS_corr` and `H1CalcWeight`.
- **Severity.** Major. G14 needs at least one concrete numerical pass criterion (e.g., "closure on inclusive DIS event rate per (Q2, y) bin agrees with the published high-Q2 inclusive DIS cross-section to within 3% on a per-(Q2,y) basis and within 1.5% integrated, computed on the actual nominal file set"). Without that, the gate is a procedural rubber-stamp.

### M8. G15 ("e- recovery") confounds production status with physics-completeness

- **Claim being criticized.** G15 says "recovered" means: (1) periods included in the chain, (2) lumi table updated, (3) period-tagged file manifests exist and validate, (4) trigger weighting consistent across periods, (5) period-split and beam-charge checks executable. This conflates "data is present" with "data is correct." It does not require that the recovered yields per period are sane against per-period inclusive DIS expectations, that the e+/e- yield ratio is consistent with the inclusive luminosity ratio, or that the per-period charged-track yields per nb^-1 are consistent across periods (a smoking-gun check that the production fix did not silently drop or duplicate runs).
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 208-219 (G15), lines 522-538 (G15 tasks). The extension research at Section 6.2 names the configuration recovery path; nothing requires a post-recovery physical sanity check.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** The recovery is described as a "configuration fix" in the supporting research. Configuration fixes have a long history in this project of introducing silent run drops (handoff "Mistakes Made #" list) and double-counting (12 missing-file episode). Without a per-period yield-per-luminosity sanity check, G15 can pass while the recovered chain mis-counts a period by 5-15%, biasing the unblocked A_h immediately.
- **Severity.** Major. G15 needs a "per-period charged-track yield per nb^-1 is consistent across periods within stated statistics" exit criterion, plus a per-period inclusive DIS yield per nb^-1 check.

### M9. Acceptance is not required to be derived in the gen-binned tree, nor to enforce one-to-one reco-gen matching, nor to require species = pi/K/p at the gate level

- **Claim being criticized.** G6 ("Hadron acceptance") only asks for `0 < eps_h <= 1` per bin. It does not require that `eps_h = N_reco_matched(gen-binned) / N_gen` (i.e., binned in the gen variable, not in the reco variable) -- which is the only correct definition for an unfolded-flavored bin-by-bin acceptance. It does not require that the gen-side species set is `{pi, K, p}` only (the species-policy gate G3 is a separate gate, not a precondition that flows into the acceptance formula). It does not require that the reco-side matching be one-to-one with no double-counted gen track.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 132-138 (G6), lines 108-115 (G3), lines 389-404 (G6 tasks). The handoff (Mistakes #1 and "Frozen Analysis Decisions") and the snapshot Section 7 do anchor the policy, but the gate text does not weave it in.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Reco-binned acceptance can exceed 1 (a known historical defect in this analysis, see handoff "GenElecPhi inconsistency between numerator and denominator in `extract_multiplicities.py` ... was the cause of efficiency > 1 in the acceptance map" at lines 1000-1002). Many-to-one matching inflates the numerator; cross-species pool contamination biases the denominator down. G6's `eps_h <= 1` clip masks all three bugs simultaneously while still passing the gate.
- **Severity.** Major. G6 should explicitly require the gen-binned formula, the one-to-one matching, and the pi/K/p species set as part of the exit criterion -- not as upstream G3 prose.

### M10. Frame and convention freeze is missing for phi_h Trento and for Q2es ≡ Q2e at high-Q2

- **Claim being criticized.** G13 says "validate high-Q2 `phi_h` convention and fit stability using the low-Q2 methodology plans as the base specification." There is no statement that the Trento convention is the frozen convention, no statement of the sign-and-atan2 formula, and no statement that the convention freezes for high-Q2 to match low-Q2 for any future combined release. Similarly, eSigma is stated as the baseline ("`Q2es ≡ Q2e` by H1CalcKine design") but the plan does not require that the high-Q2 baseline carry-over is verified on a control plot of `Q2es vs Q2e` per the low-Q2 OQ8 resolution method.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 188-194 (G13), lines 493-508 (G13 tasks). Handoff line 1009 ("eSigma: Q2es ≡ Q2e by H1CalcKine design") and snapshot Section 7 item 3.
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** A phi convention drift between regimes silently flips the sign of A1 and A2 between low-Q2 and high-Q2 tables; A1 sign is the Cahn-effect anchor and a sign flip would invert the physics interpretation. A failure to verify `Q2es ≡ Q2e` at high-Q2 leaves open the possibility that the high-Q2 upstream evls eSigma calibration produces a non-identity relation, biasing every (Q2, x) bin assignment.
- **Severity.** Major (would be Fatal if the plan were a methods paper; for a planning document it is a missing gate that has a clean fix).

### M11. Multiple agent assignments are physics-inappropriate

- **Claim being criticized.** Several Tasks assign agents whose ownership scope does not include the task:
  - **G5.2** assigns `h1-unfolder` to "evaluate whether Scheme A is viable at high-Q2 or whether coarser binning is mandatory" -- that is a binning/purity study which belongs to `h1-acceptance` (binning, purity, occupancy are standard acceptance-side concerns) with `h1-unfolder` consulted for the migration regime. As written, `h1-unfolder` would be doing the occupancy study.
  - **G3.2** assigns `h1-analysis-critic` (this agent) to "audit the reco-gen matching for one-to-one exclusivity" -- this is execution work, not adversarial review. `h1-root-analyst` or `h1-data-validator` owns this; the critic reviews the output.
  - **G5.3** also assigns `h1-analysis-critic` to "produce a high-Q2-specific bin recommendation with explicit accepted, unfolding-only, and masked regions" -- again, production work.
  - **G14.2** assigns `h1-analysis-critic` to "run a closure test showing that the normalization semantics are internally consistent end-to-end" -- this is a normalization closure to be run by `h1-normalization` or `h1-cross-section`, then critiqued.
  - **G16.1** assigns `h1-plotting` to "Assemble the first internal multiplicity package with full caveats, frozen scope, and source-by-source systematics" -- this is a packaging/integration task owned by the supervisor or `h1-cross-section` + `h1-systematics`; `h1-plotting` only renders.
  - **G17.2** assigns `h1-asymmetry` to "Include `A_h` in publication packaging" -- packaging is supervisor/integration work.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 372-388 (G5 tasks), lines 338-353 (G3 tasks), lines 510-520 (G14 tasks), lines 539-554 (G16 tasks), lines 556-571 (G17 tasks).
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Wrong-specialist routing means the wrong physics priors get applied: `h1-unfolder` will optimize binning for unfolding stability rather than for occupancy and physics interpretability; `h1-plotting` will produce a package whose internal-vs-publication scoping is enforced visually but not numerically; `h1-analysis-critic` doing production work loses its adversarial role and silently rubber-stamps its own output downstream.
- **Severity.** Major (operational), not analysis-killing if the supervisor catches it before delegation. Should be rewritten before execution.

### M12. The first-week actionable subset includes G5 task before G3 is closed

- **Claim being criticized.** The first-week actionable subset (lines 599-612) includes "Launch the high-Q2 occupancy/purity/binning study by reusing low-Q2 TMD tooling on the validated MC file set" assigned to G5/`h1-acceptance`. G5's precondition is "G3 and G4 passed". G3 includes the species/matching/source-class audit which the same first-week table lists as separate, in-progress tasks. The first-week table therefore authorizes G5 work before G3 is even completed.
- **Evidence.** Plan lines 124-130 (G5 precondition), lines 599-612 (first-week table).
- **Physical mechanism of bias.** Running an occupancy study with un-audited species and matching means the occupancy is computed on a polluted denominator (e.g., scattered electron leaking into the gen pool, or many-to-one matched reco tracks inflating the matched-gen count). The high-Q2 binning recommendation derived from that polluted occupancy is then frozen at G5 exit, and the downstream G6/G7/G8 chain inherits a wrong binning from which it is hard to back out.
- **Severity.** Major (sequencing). Fix is to drop the G5 task from the first-week subset or to gate it explicitly behind G3 completion.

## Missing Evidence / items the plan FORGOT entirely

Items not in the plan that this critic would expect for a high-Q2 SIDIS-multiplicity convergence plan, in priority order:

1. **Electron-ID purity / electron-finder validation.** Plan never mentions the LAr electron-ID purity (background from neutral pions, photons faking electrons), nor a fiducial volume check (`FidVolCut` layers documented in the extension research Section 4.1 and `research-highQ2-proton-asymmetry.md`). Without these, the DIS event selection itself is contaminated.
2. **Photoproduction (gamma-p) background subtraction.** Listed in G11 as a "tracked blocked source" but never gated. At high-Q2 photoproduction is small but at the low-Q2 edge of high-Q2 (Q2 ~ 150-300) it is not negligible.
3. **QED Compton / elastic-radiative subtraction.** Not mentioned anywhere. Standard H1 high-Q2 subtraction class.
4. **Diffractive (and beam-gas) background.** Diffractive is mentioned as a blocked source. Beam-gas is not mentioned at all.
5. **Cosmics rejection.** Not mentioned. Standard at H1.
6. **Vertex-z cut.** The handoff says `|VtxZ| < 35 cm` for high-Q2 (vs 30 cm for low-Q2). The plan never freezes this cut or includes a `vtxz_scan` systematic, even though the systematics reference (line 316) lists `vtxz_scan` as a standard handle.
7. **Empz cut at high-Q2.** Handoff says `E - pz ∈ [45, 65]` for high-Q2 (vs [35, 70] for low-Q2). The plan never freezes this. Empz is the leading photoproduction-rejection cut and changes the DIS event count by several percent.
8. **Run-quality / good-run-list freeze.** The data recovery (G15) needs to use the right good-run file per period (`nc_goodruns_*.root`). Not gated.
9. **Luminosity-uncertainty handling.** The plan correctly excludes luminosity from multiplicity totals (handoff "Frozen Analysis Decisions") but the per-period luminosity uncertainty for any normalization-bearing observable is not propagated. (The DESY 20-176 number was 2.5%.)
10. **MC statistical-uncertainty propagation into acceptance.** The plan never mentions that the acceptance carries an MC-stat error that must be propagated into the multiplicity error bar (and that this is *not* a systematic but a statistical contribution to be combined with the data-stat error).
11. **HEPData packaging metadata.** G17 produces a `highQ2_hepdata_manifest.json` but the plan does not name the required metadata fields (regime flag, dis_method flag, theory-caution flags, phi_h convention, bin edges, per-source covariance hints). The extension research Section 6.9 names these.
12. **Blinding policy.** Standard for asymmetry-class measurements. Not mentioned. The plan should either state explicitly that A_h is not blinded (and why) or specify a blinding scheme until G15 closes.
13. **NuclIA / nuclear-interaction weight handling.** The handoff (line 856) records `NucliaWeight` as a v5b branch. The plan never says whether NucliaWeight is applied at high-Q2, whether it cancels in A_h, or whether a systematic on its application is required.
14. **Combined-track inclusion sanity check.** The handoff freezes combined tracks as included. The plan should require a closure that combined-track inclusion does not bias the acceptance or A_h vs central-only, given that combined-track efficiency uncertainty (10%) is 20x the central-track uncertainty (0.5%) per the systematics reference.
15. **`x_diffr_flag` calibration at high-Q2.** The extension research Section 6.9 calls this out. The plan does not include a calibration task.
16. **Electroweak gamma-Z interference at high Q2 > 2000 GeV2.** The extension research Section 7.2 row R9 flags this. The plan does not address it; per the snapshot, Q2 reaches ~20,000 GeV2 so this is in scope.
17. **MC-version (oo-release) consistency check.** Extension research Section 7.2 row R7: low-Q2 uses oo-4.0.6, high-Q2 uses oo-4.0.25 MC13. Not gated.
18. **`ParticleSource_cg` (gen-side source class) presence verification at high-Q2.** Snapshot Section 10 item 13. The plan's G3 audits `ParticleSource_ch` (reco side) but does not require the gen-side `ParticleSource_cg` to exist at high-Q2.
19. **Broad-vs-analysis gen fiducial verification at high-Q2.** Snapshot Section 10 item 12 (whether `|GenEta|<3.5, GenPt>0.05` was adopted at high-Q2 in v5b). The plan never gates this. If the high-Q2 builder still uses the analysis-level cuts for `inFiducialCharged`, the gen denominator is mis-defined and acceptance is off.
20. **Branch-content enumeration of the 5 extra `ChargedTotGen` and 2 missing `DisTot` branches.** G2 task 3 produces a delta document but does not require that the missing `DisTot` branches be reconciled against the low-Q2 N_DIS counting formula. If one of the missing branches is the one low-Q2 uses to count zero-hadron events, that breaks G4 silently.
