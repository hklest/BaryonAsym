## Derivation

- `frac_full_template` = |relative change of the unfolded cross section| if the full DIFFVM template (generator cross section, luminosity-scaled by 0.135913) were subtracted (budget column `diffvm_rho_signed_rel`).
- `frac_datatuned` = s x `frac_full_template`, s = 0.07952 (data-tuned scale, DVS:9).
- `unc_1sigma` = sigma_s x `frac_full_template`, sigma_s = 0.14571 (DVS:9); correlation rho_ij = s_i s_j across all bins and both charges (fully correlated up to sign), since one global s drives every bin.
- `unc_ul95` = 0.42 x `frac_full_template`; 0.42 is the conservative 95% upper limit on s (DVS:11); OTC section 4 shows it also covers a doubled lost-pion topology.
- `sigma_nb` is the raw unfolded cross section before C_rad; the fractions apply equally to the Born values.
- Sign convention: the columns are magnitudes. Subtracting DIFFVM lowers the cross section in the 62 listed bins with positive `diffvm_rho_signed_rel`; in the 28 bins with `sign` = -1 the sign is opposite and subtracting raises it (largest full-template size among them 1.1%).
- All fractions are relative to `sigma_nb`, at the unfolded cross-section level.
