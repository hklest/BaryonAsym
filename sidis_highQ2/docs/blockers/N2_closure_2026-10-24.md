# N2 closure: HFS E-pz channel and the electron-energy cut on shifted legs

Status: CLOSED 2026-10-24 for the eΣ (esigmaphoton 2026-10-23) release, by user decision (D2). No rerun needed.

## What the blocker was
The 2026-10-19 hadronic-final-state (HFS) systematic shift was applied only in the final kinematics calculation. The quantity E−pz, which depends on the hadronic energy, stayed identical on the HFS-energy-scale, HFS-resolution-scale and HFS-angle legs. Events that should migrate across the E−pz > 45 GeV cut were therefore missing. The fix is the shim `empz_hfs_shim.py`, which sets E−pz to E−pz + 2·27.6·(Yh_leg − Yh_nom). One open question remained (D2, `/data/dust/group/h1/klesthen/BaryonAsym/sidis_highQ2/docs/handoffs/esigma_release_2026-10-22/reference/critic_phys_F1_2026-10-22.md` L41, L59): the fixed cut E′ > 11 GeV on the scattered-electron energy must also apply on the shifted legs.

## User decision D2 (2026-10-24, verbatim)
"Yes, that's a hard cut we have on the data". The E′ > 11 GeV cut applies to the shifted legs too.

## Evidence (read-only check, 2026-10-24; U = /data/dust/group/h1/klesthen/BaryonAsym/H1ChargedSidis_HighQ2/scripts/unfolding)
- Electron legs (E′ ±1%, electron angle): `U/esigma_frame_2026-10-22/run_prod.sh` L31 runs `U/final_detlegs_optA_2026-10-21/build_migration_th2_per_charge_cone_s2prime_shifted_optA_esph.py` (870 jobs). It imports `apply_f1` from `f1_nominal_consistent_esph.py` (builder L642, call L834). In that file, L27 sets ENV_E_MIN=11.0 and L135-146 veto events that pass E′ ≥ 11 GeV at nominal but fall below it after the shift. The veto sets the shifted E−pz to NaN, which fails the builder's E−pz > 45 cut. The original `f1_nominal_consistent.py` has the same block. The fork was saved at 10-22 23:13:59, before the first production log (23:19:38). Every production job also checks the fork's md5 against `fork_manifest.json` (`run_prod.sh` L19-26), and the current file matches that md5. So the cut is applied.
- HFS legs: `run_prod.sh` hfs case, 1044 jobs through `run_with_empz_shim.py` (N2_MODE=corr). The HFS shift does not move the electron and the shim touches only E−pz. The E′ > 11 GeV cut comes from the Stage-1 trees. In one HFS-leg tree (`/data/dust/group/h1/klesthen/BaryonAsym/H1ChargedSidis_HighQ2/output_v6/systematics/hfs/hfs_jes_down/sidistree/mc/rapgap31/7071/sidistree_rapgap31_7071_em06_0000_hfs_jes_down.root`) there are 0 rows with 0 < scatEE < 11 and the smallest positive value is 11.000077 GeV. So the cut holds by construction.
- sigmah_smear and jes_sym legs: the electron is untouched and they use the same Stage-1 MC trees. This is an inference from the code; jes_sym reads the HFS-leg outputs.
- Budget columns `hfs_jes_rel` (`phaseB/jes_sym/jes_sym_esph.json`), `hfs_rces_rel` and `hfs_theta_rel` (`downstream/detlegs/detlegs_mconly_esph.json`) come from these shimmed MC-only legs. There are 1044 `*.n2shim.json` sidecars, all mode corr.

## Caveats
- The veto count `n_envelope_veto` is computed but never logged, so the number of events removed in the E′ −1% leg is not on record.
- The cut is one-sided. Events that would move in from below 11 GeV are absent from the Stage-1 trees (documented truncation, the same for data).
- On the HFS legs only the E−pz > 45 cut acts on the shimmed E−pz. There is no 30 < E−pz < 75 recut there. The lower edge is inactive since 45 > 30; the upper edge at 75 is not re-applied (inference).
- The size of the N2 effect was never measured. An optional null-versus-corr study on Condor would take hours.
