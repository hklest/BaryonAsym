% Results section for the H1 high-Q^2 charged-particle SIDIS analysis note.
\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!8,colframe=orange!70,title={\bfseries Current status of the measurement}]
The nominal central values use the $e\Sigma$ method for the photon: $z$, $\pTh$,
and the $\gamma^{*}p$ frame follow the $e\Sigma$ reconstructed photon (built
from the electron and the hadronic final state) instead of the electron-only
photon.  The truth-level definition of the response is harmonized
to the radiative correction (Sec.~\ref{sec:unfolding:truth-contract}); the
reconstructed-level selection combines the $\alpha\ge0.2~\mathrm{rad}$
laboratory-frame scattered-electron cone with the
detector-interaction-corrected response, and the electron, V0, and
low-$\Qsq$ background templates are subtracted from the data vector before
unfolding.  The cross section is Born-level provisional:
$C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ is applied per bin after the unfolding from the DJANGOH14 table
in the measurement binning (applied as is, without recomputation in the $e\Sigma$ frame), with its uncertainty carried as the components
``QED radiative correction: model'' and ``QED radiative correction: MC statistics''.  The
$E-p_z>45~\GeV$ convention is retained.  The quoted systematic budget has median
totals of $8.3\%$ ($\hplus$) and $8.9\%$ ($\hminus$)
(Sec.~\ref{sec:syst:budget-regeneration}).  Earlier central values, from before the switch to the $e\Sigma$ photon and the
harmonization of the truth-level definition, are
superseded for the nominal and kept unchanged for reference.  The low-$\Qsq$ analysis
still uses the electron photon, a documented difference between the two
analyses.  The exposure of the simulated
radiative samples is reconciled file by file, but the generator-log
cross-check is permanently blocked externally, so the radiative correction
remains provisional and the result is not publication-grade.  The
$C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ multiplicity mapping and the
corrected inclusive DIS denominator are still pending.  Numerical
multiplicities and final physics tables should therefore be regarded as
preliminary until the denominator is available.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Nominal Cross Sections}
\label{sec:results:z005-release}

The nominal measurement contains 490 published bins in total,
with 245 $\hplus$ bins and 245 $\hminus$ bins.  The nominal unfolding method is
iterative Bayesian unfolding with four selected iterations.  The data vector
length is 980, and the per-charge response shape is $(491,246)$.

The raw unfolded cross sections and the Born-level cross sections derived from
them ($C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ applied) both use the response matrix's own generator-level
distribution as the unfolding prior, on the harmonized response of
Sec.~\ref{sec:unfolding:truth-contract}.  Integrated over the 245 supported bins
per charge at $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{data}}=350890.85~\mathrm{nb}^{-1}$, the raw
cross sections (before $C_{\mathrm{rad}}$) are $10.54~\mathrm{nb}$ ($\hplus$) and
$10.25~\mathrm{nb}$ ($\hminus$); the Born-level cross sections
($C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ applied) are
$12.02~\mathrm{nb}$ ($\hplus$) and $11.69~\mathrm{nb}$
($\hminus$).

The harmonization of the truth-level definition superseded for the nominal the
earlier background-subtracted raw cross sections with the electron cone and
their Born-level counterparts, which are kept unchanged for reference; their
integrated values
($10.51~\mathrm{nb}$ and $10.22~\mathrm{nb}$ raw,
$12.00~\mathrm{nb}$ and $11.66~\mathrm{nb}$ Born) are superseded
central values.  The harmonization moves the integrated raw totals by $+0.024\%$
($\hplus$) and $+0.023\%$ ($\hminus$) and the Born totals by $+0.0074\%$ and
$+0.0064\%$.  Per cell, the Born shifts have absolute medians of $1.1\%$
($\hplus$) and $1.1\%$ ($\hminus$), and the largest populated shift is
$+4.2\%$ (negative charge, global bin 159), below its full-sample net
straddle rate of $+4.9\%$ (ratio $0.855$); every well-populated per-cell
shift is bounded by its full-sample per-cell straddle rate, the complete
exception set being the adjudicated 48-cell list of
Sec.~\ref{sec:unfolding:truth-contract}.  The change is a
one-time central-value correction (the removal of the response binning bias
documented in Sec.~\ref{sec:unfolding:truth-contract}) and is not carried
as an uncertainty.  With the electron-only photon, the background
subtraction removes $281{,}250$ weighted tracks, $7.3\%$, from the cone data
vector of $3{,}865{,}073$ in-grid entries before the unfolding:
$148{,}976$ electron and positron tracks, $132{,}065$ V0 decay daughters,
and $208$ low-$\Qsq$ leakage tracks.  The subtracted data vector itself is
unchanged by the harmonization.  The detector-interaction class is not
part of this external subtraction: it is removed inside the response through
the per-reconstructed-bin fake fraction $f_i$ of the detector-interaction-corrected
response, and an external subtraction of it would double-remove the class.
For lineage, the immediately preceding result with the electron cone but no
background subtraction, which unfolds the full cone data vector, integrates to
raw unfolded
values of $11.41~\mathrm{nb}$ ($\hplus$) and $11.12~\mathrm{nb}$
($\hminus$); these superseded values are retained only as the historical
parent of the current result and are not current results.

The systematic budget of this measurement is described in
Sec.~\ref{sec:syst:budget-regeneration}, and its per-term medians are listed in
Table~\ref{tab:syst:z005-components}.  The earlier budgets (the one with the
electron-only photon, a twenty-active-term budget, and its
nineteen-component predecessor) are superseded as a quoting basis and
kept unchanged for reference.  The component inventory is described in
Sec.~\ref{sec:systematics}.

The central unfolding implementation has an independent numerical cross-check:
RooUnfoldBayes reproduces the in-house iterative-Bayesian yields to a maximum
relative precision of $2.6\times10^{-15}$ across 980 rows.  This comparison
covers both RAPGAP and DJANGOH response inputs and has no invalid or
zero-denominator bins.

\subsection{Diagnostic Four-Dimensional Cross-Section Plots}
\label{sec:results:diagnostic-4d-cross-sections}

The plotted observable used for diagnostic data--MC comparisons is the
four-dimensional differential cross section,
\begin{equation}
  \frac{\mathrm{d}^{4}\sigma}{\mathrm{d}\Qsq\,\mathrm{d}x\,
  \mathrm{d}z\,\mathrm{d}\pTh}
  = \frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}{\Delta\Qsq\,\Delta x\,\Delta z\,\Delta\pTh},
  \label{eq:results:four-dimensional-cross-section-density}
\end{equation}
in units of $\mathrm{nb}\,\GeV^{-3}$, since the $\mathrm{d}\Qsq$ differential
contributes $\GeV^{2}$. For the capped top-$x$ cell, $\Delta x=1.0-x_{\min}$.

The current comparison with five model predictions uses a superseded data input:
the raw result without the electron cone,
corrected to the photon-exchange Born level without the $Z$-exchange contribution.  It is a DJANGOH-only provisional diagnostic: the
four generator curves are at full Born level without QED radiation, while the data are
corrected to photon-exchange Born level, producing a known level difference of about $9\%$ by design;
only MAPTMD24 is photon-exchange only.  The comparison has not been redone on the
current cone-based observable basis, and its figures are not referenced in this
note.  MAPTMD24 covers 230 of 490 published bins, with no $z<0.05$ row and
sparse $\pTh$ coverage at higher $z$; this is the final limitation of that
comparison.  The comparison remains a provisional internal diagnostic.

which rejects tracks with $\alpha<0.2$.  The plotted observable is the
four-dimensional differential cross section defined in
Eq.~\ref{eq:results:four-dimensional-cross-section-density}, using the same 4D
bin-volume normalization.  The input is the rescaled unfolded cross sections from this
study, with $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{data}}=351~\pbinv$, the selection

The seven displayed $(\Qsq,x)$ intervals are listed in

$\Delta x=1.0-x_{\min}$, and the figures in this section were regenerated
with this convention.
The horizontal uncertainty is the $\pTh$ half-bin width.  The vertical
uncertainty is the quadrature sum of the statistical, model, and
regularization uncertainties, divided by the 4D bin volume; no additional
systematic components are included in these error bars.

The plots are retained here as diagnostic/provisional products only: they are
raw pre-Born inputs from an earlier, superseded analysis chain, without
background subtraction and before the $C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ correction.

citation, and multiplicities remain blocked because no corrected inclusive DIS
denominator is available. For the cross section the radiative correction is no

current provisional Born-level cross sections, with its uncertainty carried as the
components ``QED radiative correction: model'' and ``QED radiative correction: MC statistics'';
these selection-variant plots have not been regenerated on the current,
background-subtracted cross sections.  Final publication remains blocked by
publication-grade clearance of the radiative correction and by the

chain.

\subsection{Interpretation of Present Tables}
\label{sec:results:present-tables}

The present tables are suitable for validating the detector correction,
unfolding implementation, binning, and already-derived systematic components.
They are not yet the final multiplicity tables: the cross section now has the
per-bin $C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ applied after the unfolding, with its uncertainty carried
as the components ``QED radiative correction: model'' and ``QED radiative correction: MC statistics'',
but no corrected inclusive DIS denominator is
available, so the multiplicity mapping remains pending. A
cross-section-ratio radiative diagnostic is not a substitute for the required
$M_{\mathrm{norad}}/M_{\mathrm{rad}}$ mapping.  The appendix tables of this
note are rendered on the current background-subtracted basis; their basis
statement is given in App.~\ref{app:born-xsec-tables}.

The results should therefore be circulated externally with the following status
qualification: detector, unfolding, background-subtraction, luminosity, and
radiative uncertainties are included, the radiative ones as the
model and MC-statistics components of the QED radiative correction; the five detector components
are full-chain single-sided shifts, with the hadronic-final-state variations produced in the upward
direction only and their sign symmetry assumed, and the tracking-efficiency
component remains a data-side conservative shift; the electron, V0, and
low-$\Qsq$ backgrounds are subtracted before unfolding while the
detector-interaction class is removed inside the response, and the applied
$C_{\mathrm{rad}}$ is mixed-beam only; and the cut
variations serve only as robustness checks.  The quoted budget, with a median total uncertainty of $8.3\%$ for $\hplus$ and
$8.9\%$ for $\hminus$, is the one tabulated in Sec.~\ref{sec:systematics}
(Table~\ref{tab:syst:z005-components}).  No PID-separated
pion, kaon, or proton result is provided.

\subsection{Low-$\Qsq$ Companion Status}
\label{sec:results:lowq2-companion}

The low-$\Qsq$ charged-SIDIS analysis is a separate companion analysis covering
$5<\Qsq<100~\GeVsq$.  Its current publication-track headline is preliminary:
$\sigma_{\mathrm{total}}=463.9\pm5~\mathrm{(stat)}\pm48~\mathrm{(syst)}~\mathrm{nb}$
from the TUnfold-based treatment of all 768 cells, with $\sigma(\hplus)=233.3~\mathrm{nb}$,
$\sigma(\hminus)=230.7~\mathrm{nb}$, $\hplus/\hminus=1.01$, and
$\Ah=+0.0057$.  The low-$\Qsq$ result remains preliminary because several
systematic follow-ups are open, including validation of shifted-MC reuse with
its fiducial selection, replacement of the analytic statistical quote by the bootstrap
value where appropriate, and additional checks of source summaries and
regularization details.  These low-$\Qsq$ numbers are not inputs to the
high-$\Qsq$ measurement.
